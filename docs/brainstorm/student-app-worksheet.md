# Student App Worksheet

A worksheet for Develop the Future to help define the idea that has been transferred here for documentation.

## What?

My app helps people understand the math behind the house edge and why it makes casinos so much money. By using simple explanations and intuitive visualizations with shapes and graphs, I want to make sure anybody can pick up the app and get value from it. While other educational gambling tools and resources already exist, they are mostly targeted towards advanced players and are difficult for the average person or gambler to pick up and understand. My app was designed to pack as much of the understanding these advanced tools give you into an experience anybody can understand and enjoy. Its primary purpose is to bring awareness to the rise in gambling and how the games are rigged.

## Who?

My app is targeted to people who participate in any form of gambling and anybody else who is curious about how the math behind gambling works and how casinos use that to make profit. One of the main problems this app was designed to address was the increasing number of young people who are participating in gambling, so there is a bigger focus on the younger end of the legal gambling ages. This app is not a gambling experience, and all simulated gambling is directly tied to an educational aspect, meaning people of all ages will be able to use the app as an educational experience. The explanations are going to be as simple as possible to ensure that as many people as possible will be able to learn from it.

## Why?

In the past few years, there has been a massive boom in gambling participation, problems, and suicides. The numbers for young males have been growing even faster than adults. With the recent popularity rise of online gambling, it has never been easier to lose track of how much you are spending. These companies want you to think you can beat them and use predatory psychological tactics to give you that belief. My app will show people why winning is nearly impossible in the most objective way possible: math.

## Where?

My app is going to be a small and compact educational experience, so it will be usable with full functionality completely offline. This means after it is downloaded, it will be usable anywhere worldwide at any location. For the best experience, it should be used at home or during some form of break to ensure full focus on the app for an optimal learning experience.

## How?

My app will begin with a five stage introduction. It will consist of the following sections: a hook, Expected Value, Gambler’s Ruin, Martingale System, and a thank you. The Expected Value section will use sliders from SwiftUI and a custom rendering context for the shapes. The Gambler’s Ruin and Martingale System sections will use graphs from Swift Charts to visualize bankroll over time. After the introduction, there will be three sections in the app: Demos, Game, and Settings. Inside the Demos section, the user will be able to redo any of the three interactive sections from the introduction, but with customizable parameters. The Game section will feature a higher or lower style game with which event is more likely.
